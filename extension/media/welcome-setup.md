# Your AI in Kural

Pick one AI to start. You can set up others later and switch between them in the chat's model menu.

| AI | What you need |
|---|---|
| Claude | Your Claude Code login |
| Google Gemini | Your Google account |
| ChatGPT (Codex) | Your ChatGPT login |
| Your own model | Ollama and a downloaded model; works offline |

**Set up your AI** opens the installation, login, and test steps. Kural shows what each step is doing.
